Welcome to Duality

Every great style begins with a thoughtful conversation.

“Beautiful style is achieved through mastery of contrasting elements: dark and light, wavy and straight, dynamic and still.”

Duality Salon was built by mother and daughter Sue and Leah on one simple idea: salon time is your time. We take the time to understand your hair, your routine and the look you have in mind, then we make it happen.

Questions

Good to know

How do I book an appointment?

Book online any time through our booking page, or call the salon at (716) 998-8528.

How much does a haircut cost?

A haircut with blow dry starts at $52. Short clipper cuts start at $32 and kids' cuts (under 12) start at $26.

Do I need a consultation for fashion color or extensions?

Yes. Fashion color and extensions are priced at a consultation so we can plan the right color, length and budget with you.

Do you do bridal and prom hair?

Yes. Bridal styling starts at $75 and formal styles for prom and special occasions start at $60.

Do you work with curly hair?

Yes. Marianna is a curly girl herself and loves helping clients embrace their natural curls.

What are your hours?

Wednesday and Thursday 10 AM to 8 PM, Friday 9 AM to 5 PM, and Saturday 9 AM to 3 PM.
