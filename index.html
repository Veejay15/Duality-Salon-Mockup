Welcome to Duality

Every great style begins with a thoughtful conversation.

“Beautiful style is achieved through mastery of contrasting elements: dark and light, wavy and straight, dynamic and still.”

Duality Salon was built by mother and daughter Sue and Leah on one simple idea: salon time is your time. Between them they bring more than 50 years in the industry, and a team of stylists who share the same care and precision. We take the time to understand your hair, your routine and the look you have in mind, then we make it happen.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online any time through our booking page, or call the salon at (716) 998-8528. Online booking shows real-time availability for every stylist.

How much does a haircut cost?

A haircut with blow dry starts at $52. Short clipper cuts start at $32 and kids' cuts (under 12) start at $26. Your stylist confirms the final price at your consultation.

Do I need a consultation for balayage, fashion color or extensions?

Fashion color and extensions are priced at a consultation so we can plan the right color, length and budget with you. Balayage starts at $100.

Do you do bridal and prom hair?

Yes. Our Red Carpet Style services cover bridal styling (from $75) and formal styles for prom and special occasions (from $60).

Do you work with curly hair?

Yes. Marianna is a curly girl herself and loves helping clients embrace their natural curls. Our whole team keeps learning about every hair type and texture.

What are your hours?

Wednesday and Thursday 10 AM to 8 PM, Friday 9 AM to 5 PM, and Saturday 9 AM to 3 PM. We are closed Sunday, Monday and Tuesday.
